Ware-Care aims to focus on real-time monitoring of goods, providing warehouse managers with an unparalleled level of visibility into their inventory and the trading between the Goods.
The dashboard contains Incoming Goods, Inventory, Movements, Timeline and Reports. And to keep the accurate track of the goods trading the Timeline Sections comes in use. Along with that the Reports Section gives a graphical description of the data to help the user get a detailed analysis and updates of the Warehouse.
